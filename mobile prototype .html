Help & support

Frequently asked
How is the fare calculated?
A flagfall covers the first 10 km, then each additional km is charged at a fixed per-km rate shown before you confirm.
Can the driver take my car for servicing too?
Yes — use Car Service from the home screen to schedule a company driver to pick up, service, and return your car.
What happens if the driver deviates from the route?
Live trip sharing lets a trusted contact follow the route in real time, and you can message the driver directly from the trip screen.
